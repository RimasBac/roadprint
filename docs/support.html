RoadPrint Privacy policy

RoadPrint support

RoadPrint records your drives and turns them into route maps, elevation profiles, and share cards. Open the Map tab to see your drives together and find the roads you travel most.

Why do I tap Start Trip and End Trip?

You choose which drives to record. Tap Start Trip while parked before leaving, then tap End Trip after parking. Keep your phone put away while driving.

Will recording continue with the screen locked?

Yes. Allow location access while using the app when iOS asks. Once a trip has started, RoadPrint can keep recording in the background until you end it. iOS shows a blue location indicator during background recording.

How do I export my trips?

Open You → Your data → Export all trips. Use the share sheet to save or send your GPX and JSON files.

How do I delete my trips?

From You → All trips, open a trip, tap More, and choose Delete trip. To remove all local RoadPrint data, use Delete App on your iPhone. Files and cards you saved elsewhere can be deleted separately.
