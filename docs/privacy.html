RoadPrint Support

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy covers RoadPrint 1.0 for iPhone.

What RoadPrint records

RoadPrint records location only between Start Trip and End Trip, including when the screen is locked during a trip. It uses location and elevation to draw your route and elevation profile. RoadPrint never stores speed. It trims the start and end of the saved route and uses approximate areas in their place.

Where your data stays

RoadPrint processes and stores your trip data on your iPhone and syncs it to your own iCloud when sync is enabled. RoadPrint has no server, RoadPrint accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. We do not receive, sell, or share your trip data.

In You → Your data → Export all trips, you can create GPX and JSON files. You choose where to save or send those files and who sees any trip card you share.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default. When you’re signed in to iCloud and RoadPrint can use it, your trips sync to your own private iCloud database through CloudKit (container iCloud.com.rimasbac.roadprint). We can’t read that database.

RoadPrint marks these fields for encryption in iCloud: GPS points captured during recording, saved routes (including approximate start and end areas and the highest point), places visited, the main road, trip summaries, and trip timestamps. These fields are end-to-end encrypted when you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud.

To turn off sync, open You → Settings → iCloud sync in RoadPrint, or open iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud and turn off iCloud for RoadPrint. Turning off sync leaves any existing iCloud copy in place.

Purchases

RoadPrint Pro is an optional subscription. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. RoadPrint uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous identifier it creates for the app install. It does not receive your trips, routes, places, or your name or email, and we do not link it to your identity. RevenueCat’s handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

You can delete a trip from its trip screen, or delete all trips in You → About and privacy → Delete all data. When iCloud sync is enabled, trip deletions also sync to iCloud. Deleting the app erases RoadPrint’s local data. Delete any exports or cards you saved elsewhere separately.

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR or CCPA applies, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. You can view, export, or delete your trips in the app. If you email us about your privacy rights or for support, we use the address and information you send to respond.
