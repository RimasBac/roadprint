No login, account or demo credentials are required. This is an early recording beta with local device storage. Tap Start Trip while parked and grant location permission to record; tap End Trip after parking to process and save the privacy-trimmed trip. Finished trips appear in the list with distance, duration and places. Very short recordings may have no route left after trimming. The sample summary is a design preview and does not display the user's recorded trip. No RoadPrint server or analytics SDK is used. Cloud sync, purchases and other planned features are not part of this beta.
