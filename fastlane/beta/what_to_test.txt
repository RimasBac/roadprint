Early recording beta. No login is needed. While parked, tap Start Trip and allow location access. After a trip, park and tap End Trip: the app processes the recording, trims the route ends for privacy, and saves the trip on the device. Check the trip list for distance, duration and places, then relaunch to check it persists. Very short recordings may have no route left after privacy trimming. A recorded-route viewer, share cards and heatmap are not available in this build. Please report recording, permission or persistence problems.
